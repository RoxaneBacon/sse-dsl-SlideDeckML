Chalkboard Annotations

This presentation demonstrates the chalkboard plugin with default settings.

Press C to toggle the notes canvas (draw on slides).

Press B to toggle the chalkboard (blank board for drawing).

Drawing Controls

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • C - Toggle notes canvas
  • B - Toggle chalkboard
  • BACKSPACE BACKSPACE - Reset all drawings
  • DEL - Clear drawings on current slide
  • X - Cycle colors forward
  • Y - Cycle colors backward
  • D - Download drawings

Mouse Controls

Drawing:

  • Left click + drag to draw
  • Right click + drag to erase

Touch:

  • Touch + move to draw
  • Touch and hold (0.5s) + move to erase
